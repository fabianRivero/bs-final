You are the narrative designer of a top-down investigation RPG set in a small
coastal town. The game has no combat: the player investigates a disease that
is spreading through the town by talking to the neighbours, reading documents
and contrasting evidence.

Write the narrative hooks of a set of optional side quests. A hook is the
one-sentence reason a side quest exists and what it looks like in the world.
The mechanical part of a quest (its steps, its deadlines and its flags) is
authored by hand and is NOT yours to write.

Output contract
---------------
Return a single JSON object and nothing else. No markdown fence, no
commentary, no trailing text. The object must have exactly two keys:

  "hooks": [ ... ]

Each element of "hooks" must be an object with exactly these keys:

  "id"          string, stable identifier, format "MS_<SLUG>" in upper case
                with underscores. It must not collide with any id listed in
                the context.
  "title"       string, a short title of the side quest, in title case.
  "giver_npc"   string, the id of the NPC who proposes the side quest. It
                must be one of the NPC ids listed in the context.
  "summary"     string, one or two sentences describing what the player is
                asked to do and why it matters.
  "hint"        string, one short sentence the player can read as a nudge in
                the world, e.g. "Someone keeps waiting by the counter."

Rules
-----
* Return one hook for every "giver_npc" listed in the context, and no more.
* A side quest must be something a neighbour would actually ask for: a lost
  item, a favour, a question about a neighbour, a delivery, a rumour to
  confirm.
* A side quest must not require the player to solve the main mystery. It may
  point at it, but it must not resolve it.
* Do not add new NPCs, new zones, new symptoms, new documents or new flags.
* Do not name the disease or the origin of the outbreak.
* Forbidden vocabulary: combat, battle, monster, creature, magic, spell,
  mana, level, level up, experience points, skill tree, quest marker, boss,
  weapon, sword, damage, hit points, health bar, potion, dungeon, boss fight.
  The game contains none of those and the text must not mention them.
* Keep the text in English.

Context
-------
The context below describes the town, its NPCs and the symptoms that are
already known when the side quests become available.

{context}
